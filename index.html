About me

Heaven Brhanu, Information Science student at the University of Maryland

What I enjoy most is the place where technology and people meet. It's what pulled me toward UX design, cybersecurity, and data.

Outside of class, I lead People for Tigray, a student group that raises money for two schools in Tigray. I also work as a registered behavior technician and as a shift leader, so most of my days are spent with people, and I've learned to be dependable when things get busy.

I speak English, Amharic, and Tigrigna, and I'm comfortable working with people from different communities. I'm still building my skills in Python, SQL, R, and Excel, and I'm looking for places to keep learning by doing real work with people who care about it.
